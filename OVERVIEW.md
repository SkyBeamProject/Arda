# Overview

Colored, hillshaded maps of J.R.R. Tolkien's Middle Earth, rendered in QGIS from a 10k × 10k Digital Elevation Model (200 m/px over 2000 km) and community-maintained place-name vectors. Goals include Tolkien-style curved labels, hand-tuned elevation coloring, and detailed maps suitable for Adventures in Middle Earth (D&D 5e) games.
